Privacy Policy

FinSip - Water Tracker ("FinSip" or "the app") is built so that what you log stays with you. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device and why, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

1. The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in. FinSip never asks for an email address or password and does not know who you are.
  • Your hydration data stays on your device. Your name, age, weight, daily goal, every drink you log, your streak, settings, language and reminder schedule are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to us and are excluded from Android cloud backup; on iOS they are covered only by your own device backup (see section 5).
  • Two things leave your device. (1) Subscription information is exchanged with Google Play (or the App Store on iOS) and RevenueCat so that purchases can be verified; the app checks its subscription status with RevenueCat when it starts and resumes, using an anonymous identifier, whether or not you have subscribed. (2) The app's typeface is downloaded once from Google Fonts.
  • No ads, analytics or trackers today. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no usage data anywhere. If advertising is introduced for free users in the future, this policy will be updated before it goes live (see Advertising).
  • Deletion is in your hands. Drink history older than 90 days is deleted automatically. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, erases everything it stored.

3.2 Settings and preferences (stored only on your device)

The app remembers your choices so it looks the same the next time you open it: language, whether reminders are on and the reminder window (start hour, end hour and interval), your chosen fish, weather theme and skin, whether sound effects are on, a flag recording that you earned the Goldfish, and one-off notice flags (for example that a subscription-expiry notice has been shown).

It also keeps a small cached copy of your subscription status (tier, product, expiry date, whether it will renew, and the subscription-management link supplied by RevenueCat) so that the right features are available when you open the app without a connection. The cached record is ignored as soon as its expiry date passes, and it is deleted the next time RevenueCat reports that no subscription is active.

3.3 Reminders (local notifications only)

Reminders are off until you turn them on. When you do, the app asks your operating system for permission to show notifications and then schedules local notifications on your device at the hours you choose. Notifications are created and delivered entirely by your device. FinSip uses no push-notification service, no notification token and no server: nothing about your reminders is transmitted anywhere.

3.4 Purchase and subscription information (shared with Google Play or the App Store, and RevenueCat)

FinSip sells optional Premium and Pro subscriptions through Google Play Billing (or the App Store on iOS) and uses RevenueCat (the purchases_flutter SDK) to verify them. When the app starts, each time it returns to the foreground, and when you buy or restore a purchase, the RevenueCat SDK contacts RevenueCat's servers. This happens for every user of a build that includes RevenueCat, not only for subscribers. The information involved is:

  • An anonymous app-user identifier that the RevenueCat SDK generates on your device. FinSip never gives RevenueCat your name, an email address or any of the data described in sections 3.1 to 3.3, so this identifier is not linked to you by us.
  • Google Play purchase tokens (or App Store receipts and transaction identifiers on iOS) and product identifiers (finsip_premium_monthly, finsip_premium_yearly, finsip_pro_monthly, finsip_pro_yearly).
  • Entitlement status (Premium or Pro) and the transaction, renewal, expiration and cancellation state of your subscription.
  • Technical device and app information that the RevenueCat SDK attaches to its requests, as described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

This information is used only to verify purchases and unlock the correct tier. Payment is processed by Google Play (or by Apple on iOS). FinSip never sees, receives or stores your card or bank details.

3.5 Font download (Google Fonts)

The app's typeface (Poppins) is downloaded from Google's font servers the first time it is needed and then cached on your device. This is an ordinary HTTPS request which, like any web request, exposes your device's IP address to Google. No app data is attached to it. Google's handling of that request is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3.6 The fish scene

The animated fish tank runs in a local web view inside the app. It loads only files bundled with the app and does not fetch remote content.

3.7 What FinSip does not collect

  • No accounts, sign-in, email address or password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising SDK and no advertising identifier (see Advertising).
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health-platform data.
  • No server copy of your hydration data, and no cloud backup run by us. On Android the app opts out of Google's backup; on iOS your own device backup may include the app's data (see section 5).

4. Why we process it

  • To run the app: storing what you enter is what makes tracking, the goal estimate, the streak and the fish work.
  • To remind you, when you ask: reminders are scheduled only after you turn them on and grant notification permission.
  • To verify subscriptions: purchase information is exchanged with Google Play (or the App Store on iOS) and RevenueCat so that a purchase can be confirmed and the right features unlocked, including on a new device after you restore purchases.
  • To display the app: the typeface is downloaded once so the interface renders as designed.
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply to us.

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract with you (subscriptions), our legitimate interest in running a working, secure app (purchase verification, typography), and your consent (notifications, which you can withdraw at any time by turning reminders off or revoking the permission in your device settings).

5. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Everything in sections 3.1 to 3.3 lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account or moved to a new phone. On iOS the app's data lives in its private container, which is included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is held by Apple under your Apple account, not by us, and you control it in iOS Settings > your Apple account > iCloud > Backup.
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play (against your Google account) or by Apple (against your Apple ID), and by RevenueCat on their servers, under their own policies.
  • We operate no servers and hold no copy of your hydration data.

7. Deleting your data

Inside the app

  • Undo removes the most recent drink logged today.
  • Settings > Reset today clears every entry logged today.
  • Your display name and daily goal can be changed at any time. Age and weight can be changed by re-running the goal calculator, but they cannot be deleted separately inside the app.

Everything at once

  • Android: uninstall FinSip, or open Android Settings > Apps > FinSip > Storage > Clear storage.
  • iOS: delete the app.

Because we hold no server copy, this is a complete erasure of your hydration data, settings and reminder schedule.

Requests by email

If you want to ask us about anything processed remotely, or to delete correspondence you have had with us, email finsip7960@gmail.com. The Delete my data page explains what to include.

What we cannot delete

Purchase records held by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat are controlled by those providers under their own policies, and we cannot erase them on your behalf. Deleting the app's data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it separately at Google Play subscriptions (Android) or App Store subscriptions (iOS).

8. Security

  • Your data is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. On Android it is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer; on iOS it is covered only by your own device backup, as described in section 5.
  • There are no accounts, so there are no credentials to lose.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS; the app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • The fish scene's web view loads only bundled files.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no usage data anywhere.

No method of storage is perfectly secure. Keep your device locked and up to date, since anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

10. Advertising (not active today)

FinSip currently shows no advertisements and contains no advertising SDK. We plan to introduce Google AdMob advertising for free users in a future version. If and when advertising is enabled:

  • ads will be served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from device signals, ad interactions and your consent choices, as described in Google's advertising policies;
  • you will be asked for consent where the law requires it, and you will be able to change that choice;
  • users with an active Premium or Pro subscription will not be shown ads;

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar laws elsewhere may give you the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority.

Because your hydration data lives only on your device, you exercise most of these rights yourself: you can view it in the app (the app shows the last 7 days of drink history; older records are kept only for the streak and pace calculations and are not displayed or exportable), change it in Settings and Goals, and erase it as described in section 7. For anything else, email finsip7960@gmail.com. Please note that we hold no account or identifier for you, so we may be unable to locate information about you beyond our email correspondence; we will always tell you what we could and could not do.

12. Children

FinSip is a general-audience app. It is not designed for, directed at or marketed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or under the higher age that applies where you live, for example 16 in parts of the European Union). FinSip does not verify your age. The goal calculator accepts ages from 5 to 100; that range lets a parent or guardian set a target for a member of their household on their own device, and it does not make the app child-directed. Everything entered into the calculator stays on the device and is never sent to us. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact finsip7960@gmail.com and we will help delete it.
