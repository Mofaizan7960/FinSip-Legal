5. Hydration and wellness disclaimer

FinSip is a general wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition and does not provide medical advice.

  • The daily goal is an estimate calculated from your age and weight using general guidance. It does not take account of your health, climate, activity, pregnancy, medication or any other individual factor, and it may not be right for you.
  • Consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing how much you drink, especially if you have a heart, kidney or other medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, take medication that affects fluid balance, or have been given fluid guidance by a clinician. Follow their advice over anything the app shows.
  • Stop and seek medical advice if you feel unwell. Drinking too much water can be harmful, just as drinking too little can.
