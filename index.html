FinSip - Water Tracker

A gentle daily water tracker. Log each drink, watch your fish respond as you close in on your goal, and let optional reminders nudge you through the day. What you log stays on your device.

What FinSip is

FinSip helps you drink water regularly. It estimates a daily goal from your age and weight, records each drink with the time you logged it, keeps a streak of the days you reached your goal, and shows an animated fish whose mood follows your progress. Reminders are optional, scheduled locally on your device, and off until you turn them on.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • Your data stays on your device. Name, age, weight, goal, drink history, streak and settings live in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • No ads or trackers today. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no usage data anywhere. If advertising is ever added for free users, the Privacy Policy will say so first.
  • Optional subscriptions. Premium and Pro unlock extra fish and weather themes. They are sold through Google Play and verified by RevenueCat; every tracking feature is free.
