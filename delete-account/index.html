Delete my data

FinSip - Water Tracker does not create accounts. There is no account to delete and no server copy of your hydration data to request. This page explains how to erase what the app stores on your device, what that does not do, and how to reach us for anything else.

No sign-in, no remote account. FinSip never asks for an email address or password. Your name, age, weight, daily goal, drink history, streak, settings, language and reminder schedule are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are excluded from Android cloud backup. Deleting them is something you do on the device itself.

1. Erase data on your device

What to do, depending on what you want to remove
You want to Do this Result
Remove the last drink you logged Tap Undo right after logging, or use Undo on the History screen. The most recent entry for today is removed.
Clear everything logged today Open Settings > Reset today and confirm. Today's entries are cleared. Your name, goal, age, weight, earlier days and settings are not affected.
Change your name, goal, age or weight Edit your name in Settings; change the goal on the Goals screen; change age and weight by re-running the goal calculator. The new values replace the old ones. Age and weight can be changed but cannot be deleted separately inside the app.
Erase everything the app stores Android: uninstall FinSip, or open Android Settings > Apps > FinSip > Storage > Clear storage.
iOS: delete the app.		 All local data is removed: name, age, weight, goal, full history, streak, settings, language, reminder schedule and the cached subscription status.

Because we hold no copy anywhere else, uninstalling or clearing storage is a complete erasure. Separately, the app deletes drink history older than 90 days on its own.

2. What deleting app data does not do

  • It does not cancel a Google Play subscription. A subscription is a record held by Google Play against your Google account, not by the app. If you have Premium or Pro and want to stop paying, cancel it at Google Play subscriptions (or through the "Manage Subscription" link in the app) before or after you uninstall. Uninstalling alone leaves the subscription renewing.
  • It does not remove purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat. Google Play keeps your purchase history under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms. RevenueCat, which verifies purchases for us, keeps an anonymous app-user identifier and purchase records under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. We do not control those records and cannot erase them on your behalf.
  • It does not affect a subscription you later want back. If you reinstall the app while a subscription is still active, use "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen with the same Google account.

3. Send us a data-deletion request

If you want to ask about anything processed remotely on our behalf, or want us to delete email correspondence you have had with us, send a request:

Request data deletion

That link opens an email to finsip7960@gmail.com with the subject "FinSip Account Deletion Request". You do not need to include any personal details; tell us what you would like deleted and, if relevant, the approximate date of any earlier email you sent us.

We reply as soon as we reasonably can. Because we hold no account or identifier for you, the data we can act on ourselves is usually limited to our email correspondence with you; for purchase records we will point you to Google Play and RevenueCat. We will always tell you what we did and what we could not do.
